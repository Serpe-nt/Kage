## Covered and found clean (`tests/qa/infra/browser/p85.sh`)

- **Repeated header lines** (`link` x2, `x-multi` x3, `vary` x2,
  `set-cookie` x2) at level 2: no value holds a line feed in either engine,
  first visit and revisit (browser-01). Chrome gives one entry per line in
  the site's order; Firefox one value per name joined with `, `, standing
  where its first line came, as §16.5 documents (F190).
- **A `304` that names other fields** (`content-type: text/plain`,
  `content-language`, `cache-control`, `x-after`): both engines keep
  `text/html` and the `extract` over three visits, and take the other
  fields from the `304`, alike (browser-03; RFC 9111 §3.2 and Firefox's
  `UpdateHeaders` list).
- **Firefox's kept body read from its cache entry** (browser-05; revisit in
  a session, the site answering the validator with `304`, `include:
  ["raw"]`): `raw_body` equals the page sent, and an artifact is kept, for
  identity, `gzip`, `br`, `deflate` as zlib and as raw, `zstd`, chunked
  (no `Content-Length`), a `Vary: accept-encoding` page, the page behind a
  `302`, and a 600 KB page (611,211 characters, gzip, read whole); a
  revisit with `max_body_bytes: 100` gives 100 characters and `truncated:
  true`. Chrome's results are the same, except `br` and `zstd` over plain
  `http`, which Chrome does not ask for: the site sent them anyway, Chrome
  kept no page, and the revisit is the site's `304` with nothing kept (the
  probe's site misbehaving, not a finding).
- **A flow's `max_body_bytes`** (extract-04): a 16,891-byte page, bound
  3000: the final page and the `extract` step's capture are both
  `truncated: true`, both engines.
- **Hidden links** (extract-06): links a style sheet hides, with `{ }`, a
  backtick, `%5E`, `%7C`, `^` and `|` in the query, `^` in the path with a
  `|` in the fragment, a space, a quote: none in `extract.links`, both
  engines; the visible link is.
- **Downloads with `include: ["raw"]`** (crawl-04): typed and as a batch
  item, `ok`, reason `download`, no `raw_body`, no artifact, and no byte of
  the file in the result or the job's items, both engines; a flow's click
  on a download link stays on its page and keeps no byte of the file.

Not covered: `https` (h2) for the cache read and the header order; a
crawl's click road to a `304`; Firefox's cache entry under several labels
