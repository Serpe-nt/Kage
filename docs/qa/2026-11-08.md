## Proposed plan items

One line each; the planner assigns the numbers.

- **(high)** F116 on a crawl's click road: a link answered `204` or `205`
  is `ok` with its status in both engines, never `download`, never a wait
  for a load, and the reader keeps its tab (crawl-02).
- **(medium)** F119 for every host of the crawl's site: a sitemap on a host
  whose `robots.txt` is not known yet waits for that file under `enforce`,
  and is `robots_denied` when it disallows it (crawl-03).
- **(medium)** `soft-blank-page`'s frames exception looks at the frames the
  page shows (at level 2 the browser's frame tree; at level 0 a frame
  element outside comments, `<noscript>` and hidden elements), with fixtures
  of a blank page with GTM's `<noscript>`, a comment and a hidden pixel
  (class-01).
- **(low)** An untyped body is HTML for the crawl's links only when it
  sniffs as markup, and §13's two sentences agree (crawl-01).
- **(low)** A browser's `304` with no kept page is one result in both
  engines, as 204/205 are (class-02).
- **(low)** What a `download` result carries under `include: ["raw"]`: one
  rule in both engines, documented (crawl-04).
